# PayFix AI 2.0

## Intelligent Payment Dispute and Refund Operations Platform

PayFix AI 2.0 is a self-directed UK-style Technical Business Analyst portfolio project focused on transforming digital payment dispute and refund operations.

The project will demonstrate practical experience in:

- Business analysis
- Technical systems analysis
- Agile delivery
- Scrum
- Kanban
- Jira
- Confluence
- Business and user requirements
- Process improvement
- Data and API analysis
- AI-assisted automation
- Controlled AI agents
- Responsible AI
- Testing and UAT
- Release and implementation planning
- Benefits measurement

The project is designed against the Working-level capabilities of the UK Government Digital and Data Profession Business Analyst capability framework.

## Business problem

Payment disputes and refunds are often managed through fragmented systems, spreadsheets, emails and separate operational teams. This can result in unclear ownership, delayed handoffs, inconsistent case categorisation, missed service-level targets, slow refund decisions and poor customer communication.

## Proposed solution

PayFix AI 2.0 will explore a secure and accessible dispute-management platform using workflow automation and controlled AI assistance. Significant refund, fraud and customer-impacting decisions will remain subject to human review and approval.

## Delivery approach

- Agile will provide the overall iterative delivery approach.
- Scrum will manage planned product development through Sprints.
- Kanban will manage discovery, analysis, defects, support and continuous improvement.
- Jira will manage delivery work and traceability.
- Confluence will manage analysis, requirements, decisions and supporting evidence.
