# AI-Assisted Digital Payment Dispute & Refund Management Platform

## Project Overview

This is a Technical Business Analyst / IT Business Analyst portfolio prototype built to demonstrate how digital payment dispute and refund handling can be improved using structured case management, data analysis, business rules, AI-assisted triage, SLA tracking, dashboard reporting, and human oversight.

The project is based on a synthetic fintech/payment operations dataset and does not use any real customer, bank, merchant, or transaction data.

## Business Problem

Digital payment dispute and refund teams often face issues such as:

- Manual case handling
- Delayed refund decisions
- SLA breaches
- Inconsistent dispute classification
- Weak visibility of root causes
- Limited dashboard reporting
- Repeated manual customer communication
- Lack of structured human oversight for high-risk cases

## Prototype Features

The Streamlit prototype includes:

- Executive dashboard
- Dispute case list
- AI-style dispute triage
- Refund eligibility checker
- SLA breach checker
- Customer response generator
- Human oversight warning for sensitive cases
